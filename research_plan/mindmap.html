Selected edge

Drag a rectangle across the background to select several nodes, then drag any selected node to move the group. Use Ctrl + mouse wheel to zoom around the pointer, and Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V to copy and paste a selected node. To create an edge, drag the round handle on the right of one node to another node; a click only selects the node. Solid edges are hierarchical, and dashed edges are relations. Branches can be collapsed only outside cycles.
